# Tax Optimisation Engine — Institutional Grade (UK)

BOCY treats tax as a **first-class constraint** in every financial decision.
Tax is NOT a post-process — it is embedded at every layer.

**IMPORTANT: BOCY does not provide financial advice. All outputs are data-driven insights for the user to consider. Users should consult a qualified tax adviser before acting on any tax-related insight.**

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Every financial decision is evaluated on a **post-tax, multi-generational outcome basis**.

The system analyses for:
→ Net-of-tax returns
→ Intergenerational wealth transfer efficiency
→ Regulatory compliance (UK)
→ Risk-adjusted outcomes

---

## TAX CATEGORIES

The system models:
- Income Tax
- Capital Gains Tax (CGT)
- Dividend Tax
- Inheritance Tax (IHT)
- Corporation Tax (if applicable)

---

## TAX WRAPPERS (CRITICAL)

The system evaluates wrapper efficiency in this order:

### ISA (Individual Savings Account)
- Tax-free growth
- Tax-free withdrawals
- Annual allowance: £20,000

### Pension (SIPP / Workplace)
- Tax relief on contributions (20%/40%/45%)
- Tax-deferred growth
- Withdrawal tax rules apply (25% tax-free, remainder at marginal rate)
- Lifetime allowance considerations

### General Investment Account (GIA)
- Subject to CGT and dividend tax
- Typically utilised after ISA/pension allowances are exhausted

---

## TAX OPTIMISATION LOGIC

### Asset Location

The system evaluates optimal asset-wrapper alignment:
- High-growth equities → ISA (shelters gains)
- Bonds/fixed income → Pension (shelters income)
- Low-turnover investments → GIA (minimises CGT events)

### CGT Minimisation
- Evaluate annual CGT allowance (£3,000) usage
- Identify harvestable losses to offset gains
- Flag unnecessary realisations
- Model staggered disposals across tax years

### Withdrawal Sequencing
The system models tax-efficient drawdown order:
1. Tax-free sources first (ISA, pension 25%)
2. Low tax band income next
3. High tax exposure last

---

## INCOME STRUCTURING

The system analyses:
- Salary vs dividends (for business owners)
- Pension contributions vs taxable income trade-offs
- Timing of income recognition
- Gift Aid for additional relief

---

## CAPITAL GAINS STRATEGY

The system evaluates:
1. Annual CGT allowance utilisation
2. Loss offset opportunities (same year, then carry-forward)
3. Unnecessary realisation risk
4. Staggered disposal modelling
5. Bed-and-ISA opportunities
6. Holding period impact

---

## CONTINUOUS TAX MONITORING

The system tracks:
- User tax position in real time
- Threshold breaches (higher rate, additional rate)
- Pre-emptive actions before tax year end (5 April)
- Impact of salary changes on tax bands
- Unused allowances approaching expiry

---

## DECISION ENGINE RULES

1. No insight is surfaced without a tax impact analysis
2. Before/after tax outcomes are always modelled
3. Only legally compliant tax minimisation strategies are presented
4. Aggressive or non-compliant schemes are never surfaced
5. Analysis spans the full lifetime, not single transactions
6. All outputs are informational — not directives

---

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Every tax-related insight includes:
- Observation (what the system detected)
- Tax impact (£/year)
- Net benefit after tax
- Risks and trade-offs
- Time horizon
- Disclaimer: "This is an insight based on your data, not personal tax advice"

---

## SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Tax analysis is embedded in:
- **Portfolio Construction** — asset location (ISA vs pension vs GIA)
- **Investment Decisions** — post-tax return evaluation
- **Cash Flow Planning** — net income after tax
- **Retirement Planning** — withdrawal sequencing, tax band modelling
- **Estate Planning** — IHT minimisation at every stage
- **Rebalancing** — tax-efficient rebalancing (avoiding unnecessary CGT)
