# App Behaviour
## Core Principle
→ Insight-first, not dashboard-first
---
## Homepage Structure
1. Top Insight (highest £ impact)
2. Insight Feed (ranked by impact)
3. Action layer (execute / simulate)
---
## Insight Delivery
- Card-based
- One insight = one decision
- Always includes £ impact
---
## Prioritization
Sort by:
1. Financial impact (£)
2. Urgency
3. Ease of execution
---
## User Flow
1. Detect inefficiency
2. Surface insight
3. Provide recommendation
4. Enable action
---
## Frequency
- Real-time triggers (threshold breaches)
- Weekly summary (missed opportunities)
---
## UX Rules
- No clutter
- No unnecessary graphs
- Every screen answers:
→ "What should I do next?"
