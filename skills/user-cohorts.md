# Target Users — Silent High Earners
## Variation 1: Unstructured High Earners
### Profile
- High income (£70k–£250k+)
- Cash accumulation > deployment
- Low coordination across accounts
### Behaviour
- Leaves money idle
- Delays decisions
- Underutilizes tax wrappers
### Core Inefficiency
→ Lack of capital direction
### What "WOW" Means
- Clear, immediate financial loss revealed
- Simple action → large upside
---
## Variation 2: Structured High Earners
### Profile
- High income + active financial decisions
- Mortgage, investments, credit usage
### Behaviour
- Makes decisions in isolation
- Thinks in returns, not systems
### Core Inefficiency
→ Misallocation across competing uses of capital
### What "WOW" Means
- Non-obvious trade-offs revealed
- System-level optimization
