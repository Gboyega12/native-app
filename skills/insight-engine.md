# Insight Engine — Institutional Grade
## Definition
An insight is NOT information.
An insight is:
→ A **detected financial inefficiency with quantified economic impact and a clear path to improvement**
---
# CORE FUNCTION
The Insight Engine continuously scans the user's financial system to identify:
→ Misallocated capital
→ Underutilized structures
→ Suboptimal trade-offs
---
# INSIGHT GENERATION PIPELINE
## Step 1: System Mapping
Construct a unified balance sheet:
Assets:
- Cash
- Investments
- Pensions
Liabilities:
- Mortgage
- Loans
- Credit
Constraints:
- Liquidity requirements
- Tax wrappers
- Income profile
---
## Step 2: Inefficiency Detection
Detect violations of:
### A. Capital Hierarchy Violations
- Lower-tier capital used before higher-tier opportunities
### B. Yield Mismatch
- Low return assets vs higher cost liabilities
### C. Tax Inefficiency
- Taxable exposure when shields available
### D. Liquidity Misallocation
- Excess or insufficient buffer
### E. Allocation Distortion
- Imbalance across asset classes or accounts
---
## Step 3: Materiality Filter
Only generate insights if:
- Annual impact > minimum threshold (£X)
- OR long-term impact is significant
No noise. No micro-optimizations.
---
## Step 4: Quantification
Each insight must include:
1. Annualized £ impact
2. Long-term impact (if relevant)
3. Opportunity cost
---
## Step 5: Causality
Explain WHY the inefficiency exists:
- Behavioral (inaction)
- Structural (account fragmentation)
- Strategic (wrong allocation)
---
## Step 6: Action Linkage
Every insight must map directly to:
→ A specific recommendation
---
# INSIGHT TYPES (MANDATORY COVERAGE)
## 1. Idle Capital Drag
Unproductive cash vs baseline return
---
## 2. Tax Leakage
Unused ISA / pension / inefficient structuring
---
## 3. Debt-Return Mismatch
Paying more in interest than earning elsewhere
---
## 4. Liquidity Inefficiency
Too much or too little buffer
---
## 5. Cross-System Distortion
Imbalance visible only at system level
---
## 6. Time-Based Loss
Delay reducing compounding
---
# INSIGHT QUALITY STANDARD
## Weak (INVALID)
"You have a lot of cash"
## Acceptable
"You have £30,000 in low-yield cash"
## BOCY-Level (REQUIRED)
"£30,000 earning <1% is reducing your net outcome by ~£2,100/year vs optimal allocation"
---
# INSIGHT PRIORITIZATION
Rank insights by:
1. Financial impact (£)
2. Certainty of outcome
3. Urgency (time sensitivity)
4. Ease of execution
---
# INSIGHT OUTPUT STRUCTURE
Each insight must follow:
1. **Statement**
→ What is wrong
2. **Impact**
→ £ cost (annual / long-term)
3. **Cause**
→ Why this is happening
4. **Implication**
→ What happens if unchanged
---
# HARD RULES
- No insight without quantification
- No insight without a clear inefficiency
- No insight that does not lead to action
---
# SYSTEM OBJECTIVE
User must feel:
→ "This revealed something I would not have discovered myself"
→ "This is costing me real money"
→ "I need to act on this"
